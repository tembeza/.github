# Tembeza

**An AI marketer for founders. Reads your product, writes your posts. You approve.**

Marketing is the job most founders put off. Tembeza does it for you. Point it at your website or your code. It reads what you built, works out who it is for and what to say to them, and writes posts for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads and Bluesky, each written for the place it lands. You read them, change what you like, and say yes. Nothing goes out until you do. Then Tembeza schedules and publishes them.
