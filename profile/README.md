# Tembeza

**An AI marketer for founders. Reads your product, writes your posts. You approve.**

Marketing is the job most founders put off. Tembeza does it for you. Point it at your website or your code. It reads what you built, works out who it is for and what to say to them, and writes posts for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads and Bluesky, each written for the place it lands. You read them, change what you like, and say yes. Nothing goes out until you do. Then Tembeza schedules and publishes them.

[Try Tembeza](https://tembeza.online) · [Read the guides](https://tembeza.online/guides/) · [About](https://tembeza.online/about/) · [Help](https://tembeza.online/help/)

## How it works

1. **It reads your product.** Give it a website address, a code repository or a document. It reads what the product really does.
2. **It writes a marketing brief.** Who the product is for, what to say to them, and what never to say. You read it and correct it before anything is written.
3. **It drafts your week.** Posts for each platform, each in its own voice and inside that platform's real limits.
4. **You approve.** You read each post, change it or drop it. Nothing goes out until you say yes.
5. **It publishes and watches.** Approved posts go out on schedule, and Tembeza reads how they did. On the higher plans it also writes replies to comments, and you choose whether it answers the easy ones itself or asks you every time.

## Who it is for

Founders and small teams who built something and have no time, or no taste, for marketing. It is not a scheduler you fill by hand, and it is not a generic copy tool. It reads the product first, so the posts say something true about it.
